Ride It Out — the build as it stands today

Every screen below is rebuilt from the SwiftUI in Steady-States/ride-it-out at the values in the source — Colors.swift, the 1/6 · 3/6 · 2/6 zone split in MainView, and each view's own font sizes, paddings and radii. This is the reference I'll design against, not a proposal.

Two things I could not copy: SF Symbols (drawn here as plain geometric stand-ins) and your grounding media (shown as the empty state, which is what a new user actually sees).
